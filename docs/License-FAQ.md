# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what Companion Scan Quest's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute Companion Scan Quest source code for personal or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

Companion Scan Quest is a Quest 3 local world and space mapper that combines WebXR Scene Understanding with a local CV server.

Companion Scan Quest uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

### What freedoms do you have as a Companion Scan Quest user?

If you use Companion Scan Quest for personal or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork Companion Scan Quest?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute Companion Scan Quest under the same license.

### Can I contribute to Companion Scan Quest?

Yes. Open a pull request on this repository.

### Can I sell Companion Scan Quest?

No. Selling Companion Scan Quest, including paid access to a hosted instance, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to the rest of Companion Intelligence?

No. Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product. Most other Companion Intelligence product repositories use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories — see the org's open-source rollout tracker for the current list.

### Unsure whether your use case is allowed?

Email support@companionintelligence.com.
